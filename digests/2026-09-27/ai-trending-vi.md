### 5. **Multi-Agent Orchestration**
- buzz (+339): hive mind communication

- **Trend**: Single agents → collaborative agent swarms

### 6. **Local-First Privacy Wave**
- taOS: "offline by default, cloud by choice"

- **Philosophy shift**: Từ cloud-first sang local-first + privacy-first
